## Stability Envelope White Paper (Implementation Edition)

This white paper provides implementation‑grade architectural guidance for stabilizing AI systems as long‑horizon technologies accelerate. It expands on the original Stability Envelope research paper and details the full stability chain — from **[system prompts](ca://s?q=Explain_system_prompts_as_runtime_priors)** to **[runtime priors](ca://s?q=Explain_runtime_priors)** to the **[Stability Envelope](ca://s?q=Explain_Stability_Envelope)** itself.

As context windows grow into million‑token and multi‑million‑token ranges, downstream brute‑force constraints introduce artifacts and distort capability. The Stability Envelope defines the upstream boundary‑preserving architecture needed to prevent drift before it forms.
